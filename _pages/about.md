I am a Master's student in the [Existential Robotics Lab](https://existentialrobotics.org/) at the University of California, San Diego, advised by [Nikolay Atanasov](https://natanaso.github.io/). I received my B.Eng. in Software Engineering from the University of Electronic Science and Technology of China (电子科技大学). My research interests lie at the intersection of robotics and computer vision.
